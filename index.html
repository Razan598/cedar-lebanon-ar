About the Cedar of Lebanon

The Cedar of Lebanon (Cedrus libani) is an evergreen conifer native to the mountains of the eastern Mediterranean. It is renowned for its majestic appearance, long lifespan, and historical importance.

For thousands of years, cedar wood was highly valued for construction, including ships, temples, and palaces.

A National Symbol

The cedar tree stands at the center of the Lebanese national flag, representing endurance, strength, and resilience.

Beyond its natural beauty, it is deeply connected to Lebanon's cultural identity and heritage.

Did You Know?

🌲 Some cedar trees can survive for more than 1,000 years.
🏔️ Lebanon's famous cedar forests grow in mountainous regions.
📜 The Cedars of God forest in Bsharri is part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
